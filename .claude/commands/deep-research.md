# Deep Research Command

Conduct thorough, multi-source research before drawing conclusions. Inspired by gpt-researcher methodology.

## Research Protocol

### Step 1: Decompose the Question
Break the main question into 3–5 sub-questions. Each sub-question should be independently answerable. Never research everything at once.

### Step 2: Parallel Evidence Gathering
For each sub-question:
- Gather from multiple independent sources
- Note source credibility and recency
- Flag conflicting evidence explicitly — don't silently pick one side

### Step 3: Synthesis (not summary)
- Identify patterns across sources
- Quantify where possible (X out of Y sources say...)
- Separate facts from interpretations
- Note confidence level: High / Medium / Low

### Step 4: Validate Conclusions
- Does the conclusion follow from the evidence?
- Are there alternative explanations?
- What would falsify this conclusion?

## For UX Research Specifically
Triangulate across:
1. **Behavioral data** — what users actually do (clicks, errors, drop-offs)
2. **Attitudinal data** — what users say (messages, feedback, complaints)
3. **Visual data** — what screenshots reveal (UI state, errors shown, flows)

A conclusion supported by all 3 sources = high confidence.
A conclusion from only 1 source = hypothesis, not finding.

## Output Standard
- Lead with the most important finding, not the most recent
- Every claim needs evidence (quote, count, screenshot reference)
- Recommendations must be specific enough to implement without a follow-up question
- Separate "now" actions from "later" actions clearly
